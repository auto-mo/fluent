Fluent app icon

The app icon as a real 3D model (three.js) with studio lighting: a tilted silver capsule mic with one red tally light on the collar. It turns slowly so you can judge the form. window.exportIcon() renders the 1024 px icon.

Where it goes

Dock icon: a still frame of this mic on its tile (sizes below). For the shipped icon it gets layered for macOS's glass icon format: background, mic, grille highlight.

Animated: on the welcome screen and in About, where it turns slowly like this. It can also catch light in time with your voice the first time you test the mic.

Menu bar: a flat outline mic, because macOS requires one-colour shapes there. It becomes a tiny comb waveform while recording.
